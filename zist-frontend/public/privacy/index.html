Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026 • Effective immediately

Welcome to ZIST ("we", "our", or "us"). ZIST is accessible at https://zist-media.netlify.app. We provide an exquisite personal archive and tracking system for literature, cinema, critical thought, vocabulary, and scholarly reflections.

Your privacy and data sovereignty are paramount to us. This Privacy Policy details the types of information ZIST collects, how we use and safeguard that information, and your rights concerning your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

When you register and use ZIST, we collect only the information strictly necessary to provide the service:

  • Account Information: When registering with email and password, we collect your name, email address, and encrypted credentials. Passwords are never stored in plain text and are hashed using industry-standard cryptographic functions.
  • Google User Data (OAuth 2.0): If you choose to authenticate via Google Sign-In, we request access only to non-sensitive identification scopes: openid, profile, and email. We retrieve only your public Google identifier, verified email address, full name, and avatar image. We never request, access, or store your Google Drive files, Gmail messages, contacts, calendar entries, or any sensitive Google personal data.
  • Personal Archive Data: The works you record (books, films), associated marginalia, critical reflections, quotes, vocabulary definitions, and quiz activity.

2. How We Use Google User Data & Information

All information collected is used solely to deliver and enrich the ZIST experience:

  • To authenticate your identity securely across devices and maintain your active session.
  • To populate your personal commonplace library, dispatches feed, and reading journal.
  • To generate tailored educational insights, definitions, and quizzes using artificial intelligence.
  • To notify you of account activity, system notices, or shared dispatches when enabled.
Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance:
ZIST's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements. We strictly affirm:
  • We do not sell Google user data to any third party.
  • We do not share Google user data with data brokers or advertising platforms.
  • We do not use Google user data to train generalized AI or machine learning models.

3. Data Storage, Retention & Security

All data transmissions are protected via Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3/HTTPS). Your records are stored in secured PostgreSQL databases featuring encrypted storage at rest. User sessions are verified via cryptographically signed JSON Web Tokens (JWT).

4. Data Deletion & User Control

You retain absolute ownership of your personal data. You may export or permanently delete your account and all associated records (including all tracked media, quotes, notes, and profile records) at any time.

To request complete deletion of your account and all associated Google OAuth data, you may navigate to your Profile settings within the app or email us directly at asif.anwar@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". Upon receipt, all identifying information is permanently purged from our primary databases within 7 calendar days.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

ZIST utilizes reputable, compliant infrastructure providers exclusively for application operations:

  • Netlify: Static web hosting and content delivery.
  • Render / Neon: Cloud hosting and secure PostgreSQL database infrastructure.
  • The Movie Database (TMDb): Metadata and cover artwork retrieval for cinematic works.
  • Google Cloud & Google Gemini: Identity authentication and content contextualization.
